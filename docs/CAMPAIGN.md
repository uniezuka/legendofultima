# Legend of Ultima
## Campaign Design v0.1

---

# Campaign Overview

The single-player campaign consists of 10 chapters.

The first 5 chapters gradually introduce the game's core mechanics and unlock the starter monster roster.

The remaining chapters challenge the player using the complete roster.

The campaign serves as both:

- Story Progression
- Gameplay Tutorial

---

# Monster Unlock Progression

At the beginning of a new game, only Slime is available.

Unlock Schedule:

| Chapter Completed | Unlock |
|------------------|----------|
| Start Game | Slime |
| Chapter 1 | Goblin |
| Chapter 2 | Archer |
| Chapter 3 | Knight |
| Chapter 4 | Dragon |

After Chapter 4 is completed, the player has access to the full starter roster.

# Chapter Structure

Total Chapters:

10

---

# Chapter 1 - The Awakening

Region:
Forgotten Village

Story:

The player discovers the Die of Origins after a monster attack.

An elderly Pathbinder teaches the fundamentals of path creation and monster summoning.

Gameplay Focus:

- Basic Movement
- Basic Combat
- Mana System

Boss:

Wild Goblin Chief

Reward:

Goblin Unlocked

---

# Chapter 2 - Forest of Echoes

Region:
Ancient Forest

Story:

The player enters a corrupted forest while searching for answers about the Die of Origins.

Gameplay Focus:

- Path Placement
- Territory Expansion
- Faster Units

Boss:

Corrupted Treant

Reward:

Archer Unlocked

---

# Chapter 3 - The Broken Bridge

Region:
Sky Ravine

Story:

The player encounters a rival Pathbinder guarding a broken Aether crossing.

Gameplay Focus:

- Ranged Combat
- Positioning
- Defensive Play

Boss:

Rogue Pathbinder Arlen

Reward:

Knight Unlocked

---

# Chapter 4 - Fortress of Iron

Region:
Northern Citadel

Story:

A powerful commander controls one of the ancient Aether Nodes.

Gameplay Focus:

- Tank Units
- Frontline Defense
- Resource Management

Boss:

Iron Commander

Reward:

Dragon Unlocked

# Chapter 5 - Desert of Lost Dice

# MVP Campaign Rules

The campaign should function entirely offline.

No account system required.

No database persistence required.

Campaign progress may initially be stored using:

- Local State
- Local Storage

Database integration will be added later.

---

# Design Goals

The campaign should:

- Teach mechanics gradually
- Introduce monsters one at a time
- Increase complexity each chapter
- Support future expansion

The first goal is to create a fun and complete single-player experience.
